Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

This Privacy Policy explains how WorkHours ("we," "us," "our") handles information when you visit this website and use its calculators. We designed WorkHours to collect as little information as possible.

1. Calculations happen on your device

All calculators on WorkHours run entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. The times, hours, wages, salaries, budget categories and amounts you type into a calculator are not transmitted to our servers or to any third party by our calculators.

2. Information stored in your browser

To avoid losing your work, some calculators save your most recent entries and your preferred time format (12-hour or 24-hour) in your browser's local storage. This data:

  • stays on your device and is not sent to us;
  • can be deleted at any time using the calculator's Clear or Reset button, or by clearing your browser's site data;
  • is not used for tracking or advertising.

If you use a shared or public computer, clear the calculator before you leave.

3. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the servers that host WorkHours may automatically record basic technical information when pages are requested, such as IP address, browser type, referring page and the date and time of the request. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may collect such information for security and operational purposes under GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not use this information to identify you.

WorkHours does not currently use analytics cookies. If we add a privacy-respecting analytics service in the future, we will update this policy before doing so.

4. Advertising

WorkHours is free and may be supported by advertising in the future, for example through Google AdSense. At the time of this update, no advertising is displayed on this site. If advertising is enabled, the following will apply and this policy will be updated:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, and may opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before non-essential cookies are used.

Advertisers never receive the data you enter into our calculators, because that data never leaves your browser.

5. When you contact us

If you email us, including through the contact form (which opens your own email app), we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to respond and to improve the site, and we do not sell it. Please do not send sensitive personal or financial information.

6. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including California and other U.S. states with consumer privacy laws, you may have rights to know, access, correct or delete personal information, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell personal information. Because the data you type into calculators stays in your browser, you control it directly. To make a request about information you have emailed us, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's privacy

WorkHours is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Links to other websites

Our pages may link to other websites, such as government resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

9. Security

This site is served over HTTPS. Because calculator data stays on your device, its security also depends on the security of your device and browser.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
