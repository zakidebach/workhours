FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is WorkHours free?

Yes. All three tools are completely free, with no account, no sign-up and no limits.

Do you save or see my information?

No. Every calculation runs inside your web browser, and nothing you type is sent to our servers. To keep you from losing your work, your latest entries are saved only in your own browser on this device. You can erase them at any time with the Clear or Reset button.

How do I calculate hours worked?

Subtract your start time from your end time, then subtract your unpaid break. For example, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM is 9 hours; minus a 1-hour lunch, you worked 8 hours. The Work Hours Calculator does this for you, including shifts that end after midnight.

Does the Pay Calculator subtract taxes?

No. It shows gross pay, which is your pay before taxes and deductions, because taxes depend on your personal situation. You can add your taxes as a category in the Salary Breakdown tool.

Is overtime always paid at 1.5 times my rate?

Not always. Under federal law, non-exempt employees generally earn at least 1.5 times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek, but some employees are exempt and state rules vary. That's why the 1.5× option in the Pay Calculator is optional and labeled as an estimate.

Can I use WorkHours on my phone?

Yes. Every page is designed to work on phones, tablets and computers in any modern browser.
