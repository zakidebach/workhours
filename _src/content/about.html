About WorkHours

WorkHours makes free, fast and private calculators that help people in the United States understand their working time and their pay.

Why we built WorkHours

Millions of Americans are paid by the hour. Yet working out your hours from clock times, subtracting breaks, handling a shift that runs past midnight and turning it all into pay is surprisingly easy to get wrong. A single mistake, such as treating 7:30 as 7.30 hours, can mean a smaller paycheck. And once the paycheck arrives, knowing where it goes is just as important.

We built WorkHours to make those everyday calculations quick, clear and reliable, without asking anyone to create an account, install an app or hand over personal information. We deliberately keep it small: three tools that do their job well.

What we offer

  • Work Hours Calculator: hours worked from start time, end time and break, including overnight shifts.
  • Pay Calculator: gross pay for any pay period, with optional overtime and a step-by-step breakdown.
  • Salary Breakdown: a simple monthly budget showing expenses, savings, what's left and a chart.

Our principles

Privacy first

Every calculation runs in your own browser. We do not collect, upload or sell the hours and wages you enter. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Transparent math

We show the formula behind each result, so you can check it yourself. When a result is an estimate, such as the optional time-and-a-half overtime rate, we say so clearly and explain what is and isn't included.

Accuracy and updates

Our calculators are tested against worked examples, including edge cases such as overnight shifts, empty or negative entries and budgets that exceed income. We never invent taxes or deductions. If you spot an error, please contact us and we will review it promptly.

Who uses WorkHours

Hourly employees checking their paychecks, shift workers in healthcare, retail, hospitality and logistics, freelancers who bill by the hour, and anyone who wants a clearer picture of where their monthly salary goes.

Languages

WorkHours is available in English, French and Arabic. Pay and budget amounts can be shown in any official currency (U.S. dollars by default). Overtime explanations follow U.S. federal rules.
