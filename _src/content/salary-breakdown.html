How to use the salary breakdown calculator

  1. Enter your monthly income. If you're paid weekly, multiply your weekly pay by 52 and divide by 12. If you're paid every two weeks, multiply by 26 and divide by 12.
  2. Type what you spend each month in each category. Leave a category blank if it doesn't apply.
  3. Rename any category by typing over its name, delete ones you don't need with the × button, and add your own, such as Childcare, Debt Payments or Pets.
  4. Review your left-over amount, savings rate and the chart. If your spending is more than your income, you'll see a clear warning.
  5. Print your budget or save it as a PDF to keep a copy.

How the numbers are calculated

Total expenses = sum of all categories except Savings
Left over = Income − Total expenses − Savings
Category share = Category amount ÷ Income × 100
Savings rate = Savings ÷ Income × 100

Example: Monthly income of $4,000 with $1,400 for rent, $500 for food, $300 for transportation, $250 for bills and $400 for savings. Total expenses are $2,450 (about 61% of income). Savings are $400, a 10% savings rate. Left over: $1,150.

A simple way to plan: the 50/30/20 guideline

If you're not sure where to start, many people use the 50/30/20 rule of thumb as a starting point:

  • About 50% for needs: housing, groceries, utilities, transportation, insurance and minimum debt payments.
  • About 30% for wants: dining out, shopping, entertainment and subscriptions.
  • About 20% for savings and extra debt payments: emergency fund, retirement and paying down balances faster.

It's a guideline, not a rule. In high-cost areas, housing alone can take more than a third of income. The point is to see your own numbers clearly, then adjust.

Tips for a budget that works

  • Use real numbers. Check last month's bank and card statements instead of guessing.
  • Pay yourself first. Treat savings like a bill so it doesn't get squeezed out.
  • Plan for irregular costs. Divide yearly expenses, such as car registration or holiday gifts, by 12 and add them as a category.
  • Review monthly. Update the amounts when your pay or bills change.

Your financial data stays private

This tool runs entirely in your browser. Your salary and expenses are never sent to WorkHours or anyone else. They are saved only in your browser's local storage on this device, so your budget is still here if you refresh the page. Press Reset to erase it, especially on a shared computer.
