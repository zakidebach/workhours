Calculation results are estimates

Hour totals and gross pay are calculated exactly from the numbers you enter, but your actual pay may differ because of factors the calculators do not know about, including:

  • your employer's workweek, rounding policy, break policy and pay practices;
  • whether you are exempt or non-exempt from overtime;
  • state and local wage-and-hour laws, such as daily overtime or double-time rules;
  • bonuses, commissions, shift differentials and other pay that can change your "regular rate" for overtime;
  • union agreements and employment contracts.

Gross pay only, no taxes

The Pay Calculator shows gross pay before taxes and deductions. It does not calculate federal, state or local taxes, Social Security, Medicare or any other withholding. Pay-period equivalents assume the same hours every period. The optional 1.5× overtime rate is an estimate that applies only if you are entitled to time-and-a-half pay for those hours.

Budgeting tool

The Salary Breakdown tool adds up and compares the amounts you enter. It does not know your full financial situation and does not provide financial planning or investment advice. Guidelines such as the 50/30/20 rule are general starting points, not recommendations for your situation.
