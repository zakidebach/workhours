How to use the work hours calculator

  1. Type your start time, for example 8:00, and choose AM or PM. You can also type 8a or 8:00 am.
  2. Type your end time, for example 5:00 PM.
  3. Enter your unpaid break in minutes or hours.
  4. Press Calculate. If you change a value afterwards, the results update automatically.

Prefer military time? Switch to 24-hour and type times such as 06:30 or 18:00. Your entries are converted automatically.

How hours worked are calculated

Net hours worked = (End time − Start time) − Break

Example: You start at 8:00 AM and finish at 5:00 PM. That's 9 hours from start to end. Subtract a 1-hour lunch break and you worked 8 hours (8.00 decimal hours).

Night shifts that cross midnight

If your end time is earlier than your start time, the calculator knows your shift ended the next day. For example, 10:00 PM to 6:30 AM is 8 hours and 30 minutes. No extra steps are needed, and the result is marked as an overnight shift.

What are decimal hours?

Payroll systems and timesheets usually record time as a decimal number so it can be multiplied by an hourly rate. To convert, divide the minutes by 60 and add them to the hours:

Hours and minutesDecimal hours
7 hrs 15 min7.25
7 hrs 30 min7.50
7 hrs 45 min7.75
8 hrs 20 min8.33
8 hrs 40 min8.67

A common mistake is writing 7 hours 30 minutes as 7.30. The correct decimal value is 7.50. At $20 an hour, that mistake would cost you $4.00 for a single shift.

Which breaks should I subtract?

Subtract only unpaid breaks, such as a 30-minute or 1-hour meal break during which you are fully relieved of duty. Under federal rules, short rest breaks (usually 5 to 20 minutes) are generally paid work time, so don't subtract them. Some states have their own break rules, and your employer's policy may differ.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate hours worked for an overnight shift?

Just enter the real start and end times, such as 10:00 PM and 6:30 AM. Because the end time is earlier than the start time, the calculator knows the shift ended the next day and returns 8 hours 30 minutes.

How do I enter a 1-hour break?

Type 1 and choose hours from the menu next to the break field, or type 60 and keep minutes selected. Both give the same result.

What's the difference between total time and net hours?

Total time is everything from your start time to your end time. Net hours worked is total time minus your unpaid break, which is the time you are normally paid for.

How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60. For example, 45 minutes is 0.75 hours, so 7 hours 45 minutes is 7.75 decimal hours. The calculator shows both formats.
